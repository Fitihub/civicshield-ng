Know the process

Only INEC registers voters and conducts elections. Your PVC (Permanent Voter Card) is collected free at your registered INEC office. On election day, accreditation is done with the BVAS (Bimodal Voter Accreditation System), which verifies your fingerprint or facial data before you vote. Results are uploaded from the polling unit to INEC's public result viewing portal (IReV) after collation on the spot — you can watch this happen. No one but INEC issues PVCs, and no fee is ever charged for registration or collection.

Do

Verify your polling unit and registration status only on INEC's official portal. Vote in secret and report anyone watching how you mark your ballot. Confirm your result was uploaded to IReV before believing a "leaked" result shared elsewhere. Report vote buying, violence or underage voting to INEC or security agencies on the ground.

Don't

Don't pay anyone for a PVC, registration or "accreditation slot" — it's always free. Don't share your NIN, BVN, OTP or PVC details to "confirm" your vote. Don't trust results announced on social media before INEC's official collation. Don't accept money or gifts for your vote — vote buying is an electoral offense under Nigerian law.

About CivicShield NG

CivicShield NG helps citizens examine election-related information, understand potential digital threats and improve their digital literacy.

Why CivicShield?

Verify before you act.

Before you forward a message, click a link, or register through a form, take a moment to check it.

Protect your information.

Personal details like your NIN, BVN, passwords and OTPs should never be requested through unsolicited messages.

Learn to recognize suspicious content.

The goal is not to depend on a tool forever — it's to build judgment you can rely on yourself.

CivicShield is politically neutral. It does not endorse candidates, parties or political positions.

How the analysis works

When you submit a message, link or screenshot, it is sent to a language model that reasons only over what you provided and clearly labelled reference information. It does not browse the internet or independently confirm facts, and it will tell you when verification could not be performed.
